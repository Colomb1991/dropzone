## 3. Road safety

- The driver does not touch the phone. The Italian Highway Code (Codice della Strada, art. 173) forbids drivers from using a smartphone in any way that takes their hands off the wheel, even for a moment: for a first offence the fine is 250 to 1,000 euros and the licence is suspended for 15 days to 2 months.
- The phone stays in a fixed holder that does not block your view.
- Touch the screen only when the vehicle is stopped and safely parked. Stopped at a traffic light or in a queue is not "stopped".
- While moving, the app locks its buttons. "I'm a passenger" is only for someone who is not driving.
- An alert never replaces road signs, traffic law, orders from the authorities or your own judgement. For limited-traffic zones, markets and time restrictions, always check the sign.
